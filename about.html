About us

A space to be human.

Piece of Mind is a non-judgmental mental health practice rooted in compassion, curiosity, and the belief that every part of you is worth showing up for.

Who we are

Therapy that meets you where you are.

Piece of Mind was created for the moments when things feel too much — or when you simply want to understand yourself better. We offer individual therapy, corporate well-being workshops, and community events designed to make mental health feel a little less lonely.

We work with individuals online from anywhere in the world, and in-person in Pune. Our work is grounded in warmth rather than clinical distance — we believe the therapeutic relationship itself is a form of healing.

Whether you're navigating grief, burnout, life transitions, or just a quiet feeling that something's off — there's a place for you here. You don't have to have it all figured out before you begin.

Our values

What we stand for.

Compassion

We meet every story with warmth and without judgment.

Curiosity

We hold space for questions, not just answers.

Confidentiality

Your story is yours. We protect it with care.

Integration

We draw from many modalities so therapy fits you, not the other way around.
