Three steps to begin.

01

Book a free consultation

03

Our approach

The way we work.

Integrative

We draw from multiple therapeutic modalities to match what you need.

Person-centered

You lead. We meet you where you are, at the pace that feels right.

Trauma-informed

We hold space for what you carry, with care and without re-traumatizing.

Queer-affirmative

All identities, orientations, and ways of being are welcomed and respected.
