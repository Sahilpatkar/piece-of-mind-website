FAQ

Things you might be wondering.

Therapy looks different for everyone. Here are some common questions — and if yours isn't here, write to us.

Ask a question
How long is each session?+
An introductory call lasts 15 minutes, and a regular session lasts 50–55 minutes.
What can I expect in the first session?+
The first session is a gentle conversation. We explore what brings you here, what you want support with, and how we can work together. You can ask anything you want about the process. There's no pressure to share more than you're ready to.
How do I know if I need therapy?+
If you've been feeling low, anxious, lost, stuck, overwhelmed, confused, or simply curious about understanding yourself better, therapy can help. You don't need to have a "big problem" to start. Sometimes people come just to make sense of their experiences and thoughts.
Is therapy confidential?+
Yes. Everything shared in therapy is confidential except in rare cases involving risk of harm (to you or someone else) or legal obligations. These limits are always explained clearly before we begin.
Do you offer online or in-person sessions?+
I currently offer online sessions, accessible from anywhere, and limited in-person sessions in Pune.
Do you work with people outside India?+
Yes. Online therapy allows me to work with clients globally.
